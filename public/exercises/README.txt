Put your exercise PNG images in this directory (`public/exercises/`).

1. `leg_press_machine.png` (Leg Press Machine)
2. `flat_dumbbell_bench_press.png` (Flat Dumbbell Bench Press)
3. `wide_grip_lat_pulldown.png` (Wide Grip Lat Pulldown)
4. `seated_dumbbell_shoulder_press.png` (Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press)
5. `cable_rope_hammer_curls.png` (Cable Rope Hammer Curls)
6. `goblet_squat.png` (Goblet Squat)
7. `seated_cable_row.png` (Seated Cable Row)
8. `incline_dumbbell_bench_press.png` (Incline Dumbbell Bench Press)
9. `dumbbell_lateral_raises.png` (Dumbbell Lateral Raises)
10. `tricep_rope_pushdown.png` (Tricep Rope Pushdown)
